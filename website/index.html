Signature journey

Rajasthan: forts, deserts & tigers

A 16-day journey through India’s most colourful state. Start in Old and New Delhi, drive into the painted towns of Shekhawati, cross the Thar Desert to golden Jaisalmer and the dunes of Sam, then continue through blue Jodhpur, the marble temples of Ranakpur and the lakes of Udaipur. Slow down in holy Pushkar, eat your way through Jaipur’s bazaars, look for tigers in Ranthambore and finish with sunrise at the Taj Mahal before heading back to Delhi.

  • Boat ride on Lake Pichola to Jag Mandir
